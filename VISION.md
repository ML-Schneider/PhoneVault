# PhoneVault

People should own their digital lives.

For decades we have trusted our memories to devices, subscriptions, cloud services, and companies whose priorities may change over time.

PhoneVault exists to reverse that trend.

It is built on a simple belief:

If a memory belongs to you, it should remain accessible to you—without requiring permission from anyone else.

Every photo.

Every video.

Every recording.

Every document.

Every note.

Preserved locally.

Verified for integrity.

Organized for humans.

Accessible for decades.

PhoneVault is not a cloud service.

It is not a subscription.

It is not a platform that owns your data.

It is a tool that gives ownership back to the people who created it.

## Principles

- Local first.
- Offline by default.
- Human-readable archives.
- Cryptographic verification.
- Open source.
- Privacy without compromise.

## Motto

**Own your memories. Not a subscription.**
